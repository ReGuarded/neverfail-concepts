What this section does
This is the bridge from reputation/pillars to Engine. Five real-world deployment scenarios — each showing a life-or-death consequence and how Engine prevents it. Closes with the transition into the Engine product sections.
Section Eyebrow
Real Environments. Real Consequences.
Section Headline
When the stakes are this high, there is only one choice.
Section Subhead
These aren't hypothetical scenarios. They are live deployments — running right now, around the world, around the clock. Neverfail is behind every single one.
Five Scenario Cards
CentralSquare · E911 Computer-Aided Dispatch
It's 2:47am. A dispatcher receives a mass casualty call. The E911 computer-aided dispatch system is the only thing standing between that call and every first responder in the city.
Engine detects a critical service anomaly and fails over in under 10 seconds — before the queue freezes, before a single call drops, before any dispatcher looks up from their screen.
The ambulances are already moving. Nobody notices.
Schneider Electric · SecurityExpert
35 million passengers move through Boston Logan Airport every year. Every access door, every security checkpoint, every restricted zone runs on Schneider Electric's SecurityExpert platform.
Engine keeps SecurityExpert continuously available — no reboot windows, no failover gaps, no exposure. When a dependency fails at the application layer, Engine remediates before a single door goes offline.
The doors open. The doors close. Nobody notices.
Clever Devices · CleverCAD
A major city's entire transit and emergency vehicle dispatch network runs on a single mission-critical platform. Ambulances, fire, transit — all routed through one system, serving millions of daily riders and responders.
Engine monitors the application stack in real time. When a dependency fails at the OS layer, it remediates locally — in seconds, before dispatch goes dark, before a single emergency vehicle loses its route.
Every vehicle gets its route. Every call gets answered. Nobody notices.
Community Health Group · Critical Apps
Inside a regional health system, clinical staff depend on mission-critical Windows applications for patient records, medication routing, and care coordination. A failure at 6am means delayed care for hundreds of patients.
Engine replicates continuously and fails over automatically — zero data loss, zero application restart, zero patient impact. The passive node assumes active identity in seconds, invisibly.
The morning shift starts on time. The records are there. Nobody notices.
Honeywell · Pro-Watch
A global enterprise's entire physical security infrastructure — access control, surveillance, threat detection — runs on Honeywell Pro-Watch across dozens of facilities worldwide. An application outage means uncontrolled access.
Engine watches the Pro-Watch application stack around the clock. When performance counters signal degradation, Engine remediates before a single access point goes offline — before security is compromised.
Every door is controlled. Every camera is live. Nobody notices.
